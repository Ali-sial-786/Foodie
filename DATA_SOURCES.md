# Data sources

| Source | Use | Licence | Shipped in app? |
|---|---|---|---|
| USDA SR28 | Reference nutrients for ingredients and the full search table | Public domain (U.S. Government work) | Yes |
| Open Food Facts | Barcode product lookups at runtime | ODbL 1.0 (database), DbCL (contents) | No (cached per product after lookup) |
| Nouri-authored dish compositions | Typical nutrition for ~85 regional dishes | Project code | Yes (calculated from USDA ingredients) |
| Approximate fallbacks | Foods missing from SR28 | Project code | Yes, flagged `APPROXIMATE` |

## USDA SR28

- **What:** USDA National Nutrient Database for Standard Reference, Release 28 (2015, revised 2016), ASCII files `ABBREV.txt` and `FOOD_DES.txt`. Committed unmodified in [data/usda-sr28/](data/usda-sr28/README.md), which also lists their SHA-256 hashes.
- **How it was obtained.** The sandbox could not reach usda.gov. The files were taken from the npm package `fda-nutrient-database@1.0.2`, whose registry integrity hash was verified and which redistributes the SR28 files unchanged. Values were spot-checked against well-known records: #20045 cooked white rice 130 kcal, #01123 raw egg 143 kcal, #04053 olive oil 884 kcal. Anyone with internet access can re-verify against the official download at https://fdc.nal.usda.gov/download-datasets.
- **Derived files**, generated by `scripts/generate_food_data.py` (`--check` verifies they are current; `scripts/fixtures.sh` runs both):
  - `shared/…/fooddb/generated/UsdaCoreFoods.kt`: the 460 records referenced by the ingredient ontology and dish catalogue, compiled in so the engine works offline on every platform.
  - `app/src/main/assets/fooddb/usda_sr28.tsv`: the full table (about 8,400 rows, baby foods excluded) for search.
- **NDB references were verified** against `FOOD_DES.txt`. Several wrong numbers caught during development were corrected (for example urad dal and teff).
- **Planned:** migrate to SR Legacy (April 2018, minor corrections) and/or FoodData Central Foundation Foods.

## Open Food Facts

- `OpenFoodFactsService` calls `GET https://world.openfoodfacts.org/api/v2/product/<GTIN>.json` with a descriptive User-Agent, as Open Food Facts asks. **Only the barcode number is sent.**
- Lookups can be turned off (`PrivacySettings.barcodeOnlineLookup`). A found product is cached locally as a user food so later scans work offline.
- Attribution text "Data from Open Food Facts (openfoodfacts.org), ODbL" is stored with each product (`OpenFoodFactsMapper.ATTRIBUTION`) and shown in the app.
- Crowd-sourced data can be wrong or incomplete. Products missing energy go to the "scan the label" flow instead of logging 0 kcal.

## Dish compositions

`dishes/DishCatalog.kt` contains Nouri's own write-ups of widely known dishes: ingredient grams and the batch's cooked weight. They are not reproductions of published recipes. Nutrition is **calculated** from USDA ingredient data, labelled "Typical recipe" in the app, and always carries a variability note because real preparations differ.

## Approximate fallbacks

`fooddb/FoodDatabase.kt` → `ApproximateFoods`:

- `approx:paneer`: typical full-fat paneer, from commercial labels (range 260–320 kcal/100 g)
- `approx:gochujang`: typical commercial gochujang

These are `DataQuality.APPROXIMATE` with `APPROXIMATE_FALLBACK` authority. Every calculation that uses them lowers confidence and adds a note. Replace them with authoritative records when available.

## Never invented

Recipes and AI-generated content never cite sources that don't exist. AI-generated recipes are labelled as AI-generated, and their nutrition is recalculated by the engine.
